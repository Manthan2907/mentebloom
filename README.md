# 🧠 MindMate – Student Mental Wellness Platform

## 📌 Overview

MindMate is a mental wellness platform designed specifically for college students. It helps students manage academic stress, build healthy habits, track their mood, and stay motivated through a simple and student-friendly interface.

---

## ✨ Features

* 😊 Daily Mood Check-in
* 💬 Motivational Quotes
* 📚 **Subject-wise Stress Tracker** – Organize tasks by subject and calculate academic stress based on workload, deadlines, and pending assignments.
* ✅ Habit Tracker
* 📅 Task & Assignment Management
* 📊 Stress Dashboard
* 📈 Progress Analytics
* 🔔 Smart Reminders
* 📱 Responsive Design for Desktop & Mobile

## 🎯 Problem Statement

College students often experience stress due to assignments, exams, project deadlines, and balancing academics with personal life. Existing mental health apps are mostly generic and do not focus on academic workload.

MindMate bridges this gap by combining mental wellness tools with academic stress management in one platform.

---

## 💡 Unique Feature

**Subject-wise Stress Tracker:** Students organize tasks under each subject, and the app calculates an academic stress score based on pending work, task difficulty, and upcoming deadlines, helping them manage their workload effectively.
